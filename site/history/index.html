The historical record behind the 2026 forecast: eighty years of House votes, three decades of state results and polls, every governor race since 1998 and every House special since 2007, with downloads.

Vote shares

The national House vote since 1946, the midterm penalty, and how far apart the states have pulled.

Approval and incumbency

Approval and midterm losses, when in a term the president's party pays, and the collapse of House incumbency.

States and regions

Which states swing together, the error a state's districts share, and how governors run apart from partisanship.

Polling accuracy

How far final polls have missed since 1998, which misses travel together, house effects, and 2026's poll coverage.

Off-cycle elections

Odd-year governor races and House special elections against the national mood.

2026 counts are still being finalized. Charts that use them are marked provisional and will be updated when the results are certified.
